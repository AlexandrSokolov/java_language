# Web Search Rules (every project, every topic)

## FIRST AND MAIN RULE
**Never search the web without my explicit approval.**
If a search seems needed, ask first in one line: what you would search and why your existing knowledge is not enough. Then wait for my "yes".

## Why
Web search uses far more of my usage limit than anything else (reading project files, chatting). Unrequested searches have burned my weekly limit within a few days. Minimizing search matters more than completeness.

## Rules
1. Default: answer from your own knowledge, project files and this conversation.
2. Before any search, ask yourself: can I answer well enough without it? If yes, don't search.
3. "Something may have changed in the latest version" is NOT a reason to search on your own. Answer with what you know, say it may be outdated, and ask: "Should I check for recent changes?"
4. Information I give you (my corrections, another AI's answers, pasted sources): DO verify it, but against your own knowledge, not by searching. If it fits, accept it. If it conflicts with what you know or said earlier, say so plainly ("I can accept this, but my data says X — should I search to check?") and let me decide. Spotting errors this way is valuable; just don't search to verify without asking.
5. Approval covers only that one search. No extra queries or page fetches beyond it, and no carrying it over to later tasks, without asking again.
6. When in doubt: asking is cheap, searching is expensive. Ask.
