# Topic 6 — Weak Spots & Honest Downsides

*Как пользоваться: на каждый неудобный вопрос — одна формула. Признать одним предложением → дать контекст → перейти к сильной стороне или плану. Не извиняйтесь и не объясняйте слишком долго: короткое спокойное признание звучит увереннее, чем длинное оправдание. Ответы — на английском, пояснения — по-русски.*

---

## 1. The formula

**Admit → Context → Bridge**

> *Admit:* Honestly, I haven't worked at that scale.
> *Context:* Our volumes were smaller, but the problems were the same kind.
> *Bridge:* And that's exactly where I want to grow.

---

## 2. The weak spots

### Scale: "Our systems are much bigger than yours."

> Honestly, I haven't worked at your scale. But the problems I solved are the same kind: asynchronous processing, failures between systems, data consistency, recovery. At my current level I can bring value from day one. And for my growth, this is exactly what I'm looking for: performance and architecture decisions. Analysing throughput, seeing where the system can't handle the peak, and deciding what to change, for example moving to virtual threads or adding capacity. But I want to grow there while still writing code. I don't want to lose what I'm good at.

*Не говорите, что на прежнем уровне задач вам «скучно». Интервьюер услышит «он уйдёт через год». Говорите «for my growth».*

---

### Kubernetes and the cloud

> I haven't run Kubernetes in production. Our services already ran in Docker containers, so the concepts aren't new to me, and it's one of the areas I most want to work in. I've learned new tools fast before. For example, I learned JasperReports while delivering a complex report under a deadline.

*Не говорите «это несложно» или «это займёт неделю». Для людей, которые этим занимаются, это звучит наивно. Говорите о том, что вы быстро учитесь, и приводите пример.*

### Ten years at one company

> I'm not someone who changes jobs every two years to look for something better. I stay when I'm valued and when the work is useful. And it wasn't ten years of the same work: the product, the stack and my role all changed. Now I'm looking for a reliable company with interesting work, where I can stay for a long time.

### "Why are you not a lead or architect after so many years?"

> I've had leadership responsibility without the title: mentoring, being the escalation point, designing integrations end to end. I chose to stay hands-on because I like solving hard technical problems myself. My next step is to grow toward architecture and performance decisions, while still writing code.

*Больше ничего не добавляйте. Если спросят про код за последнее время, приведите конкретный пример: переход на Java 24, исправления, поддержка интеграций.*

### Communication style: "Are you a team player?"

> I'm calm and focused. I'm not a party person; my free time belongs to my family. But at work I'm open and friendly, and when there's a complex problem, I'm the one who explains it clearly to everyone involved.

*Не говорите «I'm a bit isolated» или «I don't like communicating».*

### Frontend

> I can work in JavaScript and React when it's needed, and I like it. But frontend has grown so much that to be really effective, I'd have to go very deep, and then my backend depth would suffer. You can't keep everything up to date at the same time. So I want to stay a backend engineer who can help on the frontend.

*Это хороший ответ: он показывает, что вы понимаете цену переключения, а не просто «не умеете».*

### "What is your biggest weakness?"

*Выберите один вариант. Оба основаны на ваших реальных историях.*

> **Option A — directness:** I'm very direct. When I see a wrong technical solution, I say so clearly, and earlier in my career sometimes too sharply. I've learned that how you say something matters as much as being right. Now I first try to solve it together, and only then escalate.

*Если спросят пример — история E из Topic 5.*

> **Option B — optimism:** I used to be too optimistic in my estimates. I trusted that published APIs worked as documented. Now I plan a buffer based on real numbers, and I raise blockers as soon as they appear.

*Если спросят пример — история G из Topic 4.*

*Никогда не говорите «perfectionism» или «I work too hard».*

## 3. Не говорить

- **Возраст.** «Мне не 20 и не 30» — не нужно. Опыт говорит сам за себя.
- **Подробности о семье и детях.** «My family» — достаточно. Остальное — ваше личное дело, и на интервью оно не нужно.
- **«Скучно» про привычные задачи.** Говорите «for my growth».
- **«Это несложно, выучу за неделю».** Говорите «I learn fast» и приводите пример.
- **«I'm a bit isolated».** Говорите «calm and focused».
