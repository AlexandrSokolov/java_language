Chapter 6, “The Relevance of Reactive Java in Light of Virtual Threads”
In this chapter, we aim to understand the differences in
approaching concurrency between virtual threads and
reactive programming. We examine the differences between
blocking and nonblocking I/O, event-driven architecture,
and the trade-offs associated with each approach. This
chapter will help you make informed choices about when to
utilize virtual threads versus reactive frameworks.
